# data/

This folder is intentionally empty in the repository.

- `manuals/` is where you place your own copies of the factory service manuals
  and parts catalog. They are copyrighted by their owners and are **not**
  redistributed here.
- `derived/` holds files generated from those manuals (extracted text, DTC
  tables, parts database). They are generated locally and git-ignored for the
  same reason.
